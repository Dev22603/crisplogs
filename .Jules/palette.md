## 2026-05-30 - Rounded Corners for Logging Output
**Learning:** Terminal-based UIs benefit from visual softening just as much as web UIs. The square box characters (\u250c, etc.) look rigid, while the rounded box characters (\u256d, etc.) provide a friendlier, more modern appearance in the console without breaking any layout constraints.
**Action:** Always consider rounded variants for ASCII/Unicode borders when designing terminal outputs.
