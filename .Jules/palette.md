## 2025-01-20 - Terminal Box UI Softness
**Learning:** Hard angles and straight corners in terminal UI (like standard ┌ ┐) can feel dated, technical, and visually aggressive. Switching to rounded corners (╭ ╮) provides a much softer, modern, and accessible feel without altering the layout or structural clarity of the logged content.
**Action:** When designing or updating terminal UIs or logging libraries, prefer rounded box drawing characters for borders/decorations to provide a more refined and approachable developer experience.
