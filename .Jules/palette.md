
## $(date +%Y-%m-%d) - Modernize Box Styles in Terminal Logging
**Learning:** Hard angles and sharp corners (like `┌`, `┐`, `└`, `┘`) can feel slightly dated or overly rigid in modern CLI tools. By swapping these characters for their softer, rounded equivalents (`╭`, `╮`, `╰`, `╯`), the terminal output feels more polished, approachable, and modern without sacrificing any structure or taking up additional vertical/horizontal space.
**Action:** When designing or maintaining terminal UI (TUI) components or text-based boxes, default to rounded corners where supported to provide a softer, more modern aesthetic that aligns with contemporary UI design trends.
