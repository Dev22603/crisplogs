## 2025-05-18 - Rounded Box Corners
**Learning:** Terminal utilities with box drawing features often use sharp square corners (`┌`, `┐`, `└`, `┘`) by default, which can feel harsh and dated.
**Action:** Replace square corners with rounded corners (`╭`, `╮`, `╰`, `╯`) to provide a softer, more modern aesthetic and improve the visual experience of reading log outputs in a terminal.
